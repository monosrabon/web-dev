🍫 Chocolate Cake Recipe

This is a simple recipe page built with HTML. It shows ingredients, steps, and nutrition info.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix all dry ingredients.
  3. Add eggs and milk, stir until smooth.
  4. Pour into a baking pan.
  5. Bake for 35 minutes.
